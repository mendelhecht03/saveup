SaveUp.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

SaveUp is a personal budgeting web application operated by Menachem Mendel Hecht, an independent developer in the United States ("SaveUp," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information SaveUp collects when you use the service, how it is used, and the choices you have. SaveUp is a budgeting tool. It is not a bank, and it cannot move money.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and the information needed to sign you in.

Financial account data, through Plaid. If you choose to connect a bank or credit card account, that connection is made through Plaid Inc. ("Plaid"). We receive from Plaid: account names, types, and masked account numbers; current balances; and transaction history, including amounts, dates, merchant names, and whether a transaction is pending or posted. By connecting an account, you also agree to Plaid's own privacy policy, which governs Plaid's handling of your information.

Your bank credentials are never collected by SaveUp. You enter your bank username and password only within Plaid's secure flow at your financial institution. Those credentials never pass through SaveUp's systems and are never stored by us.

Information you create in the app. Budgets and budget categories, categorizations and notes you attach to transactions, planned purchases, and purchases you enter manually (such as cash spending). If you use receipt features, receipt images you upload and the text extracted from them.

Basic technical information. Server logs such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps, kept for security and troubleshooting.

How we use your information

  • To provide the budgeting features of the service: syncing your transactions, displaying balances, and calculating budget usage.
  • To maintain and secure your account, and to respond to your requests.
  • To improve the service in aggregate — for example, understanding which features are used. We do not build advertising profiles.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use your financial data for marketing.

Who we share information with

Only the service providers required to run SaveUp, and only to the extent needed: Plaid, for account connection and transaction data; and our hosting and database providers, which store the service's data. These providers process information on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Security

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted (TLS) connections.
  • Stored data, including Plaid access tokens, is encrypted at rest and kept on servers, never on your device or in the app's downloadable code.
  • Administrative access to SaveUp's systems requires multi-factor authentication and is limited to the operator.
  • No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information with safeguards proportionate to its sensitivity.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information only while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can disconnect a financial account at any time from within SaveUp; disconnecting removes the connection through Plaid and deletes that account's stored transactions and tokens from our systems within 30 days. To delete your entire account and all associated data, email us at the address below and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Your choices

  • You can use SaveUp without connecting any financial account, by entering purchases manually.
  • You can disconnect any connected account, or revoke SaveUp's access through your bank, at any time.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or its correction or deletion, by emailing us.

Children

SaveUp is a financial management tool intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above, and where appropriate notify account holders by email before the changes take effect.
