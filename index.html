A calmer way to keep a budget.

Most budgeting apps fail for a simple reason: the numbers in the app stop matching the numbers in your bank account, and you stop trusting both. SaveUp is built around fixing that. It connects to your accounts, gathers everything into one weekly inbox, and lets you decide what each purchase actually was — so the budget you see is the budget that's real.

Currently in development — early access opening soon

How it works

1

Connect your accounts

Link your bank and credit cards through Plaid. The connection is read-only — SaveUp can see transactions and balances, and can never move money.

2

Review your week

Every purchase lands in a weekly inbox, posted and pending clearly marked as such. You go through it once a week and file each item into a budget. Nothing is guessed on your behalf.

3

Budgets that stay honest

See how much of each budget you've actually used — including money you've set aside for things you're planning to buy, shown separately so it never distorts the picture.

Built for people who've tried budgeting apps before

  • Custom budgets on your schedule — weekly, monthly, or yearly limits for the categories you actually spend on, from groceries to travel.
  • Transfers are not income until you say so — a Zelle or Venmo payment is never counted as income or an expense until you confirm what it was. Money you owe yourself doesn't become a windfall.
  • Pending means pending — a purchase that's still settling is shown as pending everywhere it appears, so a large charge never gives you a misleading picture of where you stand.
  • Planned purchases, priced honestly — saving up for something? Log it with a price range, and watch unspent budget money build toward it.
  • Cash counts too — purchases that never touch a card can be entered by hand and marked as unverified until a matching transaction appears.
  • The app learns your habits — categorize a purchase once, and similar purchases are suggested for the same budget next time. You always confirm.

Your bank connection, handled carefully

SaveUp uses Plaid — the same service behind many major finance apps — to connect to your financial institution. You sign in to your bank on your bank's own secure flow inside Plaid. Your bank username and password never pass through SaveUp and are never stored by us.

The connection is read-only. SaveUp receives your account balances and transaction history, and nothing else. All data travels over encrypted connections, stored data is encrypted at rest, and you can disconnect an account at any time — disconnecting deletes the stored data for that connection.
